Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Play XI's optional Gameweek unlock is a one-time digital purchase. It does not renew automatically, so there is no subscription to cancel.

Request a refund

For a full refund, email vinay2027@mastersunion.org within seven days of purchase. Include the email address used at checkout and the FPL Team ID or Gameweek so we can locate the purchase. Do not email card or bank details.

Problems with access

If payment succeeded but the extra ideas did not unlock, contact us at the same address. We will check the order and either restore access or arrange a refund. Refunded unlocks are removed from the account.

How refunds are returned

Approved refunds are sent through the payment processor to the original payment method. Your bank or payment provider controls when the money appears in your account. This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.
